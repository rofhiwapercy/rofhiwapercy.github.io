CURRICULUM

What you'll learn

The program covers the essential areas of chess while gradually developing your ability to think independently.

♘
01

Opening Principles

Learn how to develop your pieces, control the centre, protect your king and start games with purpose.

♠
02

Middle Game & Tactics

Study tactical patterns, calculation and practical decision-making during the most complex part of the game.

♖
03

Endgames

Build confidence in fundamental endgame positions and learn how to convert advantages into wins.

♗
04

Game Analysis

Review games to identify mistakes, missed opportunities and recurring patterns in your play.

♚
05

Strategy

Develop a deeper understanding of positional ideas, planning and long-term decision-making.
